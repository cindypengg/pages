Petrimphony

Animated short, 2025

A group of music making creatures form a band... What happens at their first show?? Hahahahahaha...

This was a film that I was lost with for a long time... until GLITTER came along!!! GLITTER FREED ME!! I manipulated how it looked with the lights on a downshooter, then layered and comped it in After Effects. This film came from a feeling of loneliness, and trying not to accept change. My first time composing some sort of music, I used a lot of silly kids instruments.
